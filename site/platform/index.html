How it works

Extract. Load. Transform. Activate.

  1. 01

    Extract

    Connectors read from SAP, D365, Salesforce, NetSuite, databases and files with minimal source impact.

  2. 02

    Load

    Data lands incrementally in Snowflake, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, S3 or ADLS, in the order you define.

  3. 03

    Transform

    Python routines cleanse and enrich in flight; heavy modeling stays in the warehouse.

  4. 04

    Activate

    Reverse ETL writes modeled data back to Salesforce and operational systems.
