What is ELT?

Extract, load, transform, and why loading before transforming suits the cloud.

Extract

Data is read from source systems such as SAP S/4HANA, Dynamics 365, Salesforce, NetSuite, databases and files, with as little load on those systems as possible.

Load

Raw data lands in a cloud warehouse or lake such as Snowflake, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, Amazon S3 or ADLS. Loading first keeps a full-fidelity copy and decouples ingestion from modeling.

Transform

Transformation then runs where compute is elastic: in the warehouse, or with a Python step during the pipeline for cleansing and enrichment that shouldn't wait.

Why ELT over ETL

Cloud platforms make storage cheap and compute elastic. ELT lets teams re-model historical data without re-extracting it, and keeps pipelines simpler to operate.
